
# 🧠 **NeuroSense – AI-Powered Stress Detection & Management Platform** 🌿💖  

## 🚀 **Project Overview**  
Stress is a **silent killer** that affects mental, emotional, and physical health. 🏥💔 **NeuroSense** is an **AI-powered web application** that detects stress levels using **physiological signals** and provides **personalized stress management techniques** through an **intelligent chatbot**. 💬🤖  

Using **Machine Learning & OpenAI GPT-4 Turbo**, the chatbot offers **real-time stress analysis** and suggests techniques such as **meditation, breathing exercises, and lifestyle changes**. 🌱✨  

NeuroSense classifies stress into three levels:  
✅ **Low Stress** 🧘 – You're relaxed & in control.  
⚠️ **Medium Stress** 🔄 – Moderate stress detected. Consider taking a break!  
❌ **High Stress** 🔥 – Immediate action needed!  

## 🎯 **Key Features**  
🔹 **Real-time Stress Detection** – AI-based stress level classification.  
🔹 **AI Chatbot 🤖** – Provides instant stress management advice.  
🔹 **Interactive Dashboard 📊** – Visualize stress trends over time.  
🔹 **Personalized Stress Relief Techniques** – Meditation, breathing exercises, and relaxation tips.  
🔹 **Wearable Sensor Integration 📡** – Supports smartwatches & fitness bands.  
🔹 **Web-Based Interface 🌐** – Built using Flask, HTML, and CSS.  

## 📊 **Physiological Indicators Used**  
NeuroSense collects **real-time biosignals** from wearable devices & sensors, including:  
📡 **Acceleration (X, Y, Z directions)** – Tracks body movements.  
🌡 **Body Temperature** – Monitors temperature fluctuations due to stress.  
⚡ **Electromyography (EMG)** – Detects muscle tension.  
❤️ **Electrocardiogram (ECG)** – Measures heart rate variability.  
🖐 **Electrodermal Activity (EDA)** – Tracks sweat gland activity.  
💨 **Respiratory Rate** – Analyzes breathing patterns for stress detection.  

## 🏗 **Tech Stack**  
🧠 **Machine Learning Models:** Random Forest, SVM, Neural Networks.  
💬 **Chatbot:** OpenAI GPT-4 Turbo (Integrated for stress-related queries).  
📊 **Data Processing:** Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn.  
📈 **Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn.  
🌐 **Web Development:** Flask, HTML, CSS.  
🐍 **Programming Language:** Python.  

## 🔍 **How It Works?**  
1️⃣ **Data Collection** – Sensors gather real-time physiological signals.  
2️⃣ **Feature Extraction** – Biosignals are processed for stress analysis.  
3️⃣ **ML Model Prediction** – Classifies stress levels into **Low, Medium, or High**.  
4️⃣ **Chatbot Consultation 🤖** – Users can chat with AI for stress relief techniques.  
5️⃣ **Personalized Suggestions** – Meditation, breathing exercises, and self-care tips.  
6️⃣ **Interactive Dashboard** – Users track stress levels and improvements over time.  

## 📈 **Performance Metrics**  
✔️ **Accuracy Score** – Evaluates stress detection performance.  
✔️ **F1-Score & Precision** – Ensures balanced classification.  
✔️ **Confusion Matrix** – Analyzes prediction distribution.
